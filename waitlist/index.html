No more…

  • No more “how to talk to your husband about your mental load”.
  • No more “30 organisation tips” that only add to the overwhelm.
  • No more 10 apps for every little thing.
  • No more making lists.

Any stray thought, even half-formed, goes in here.

Emails, to-dos, goals, the meal plan, chores, that thing you remembered in the shower. It all goes in, and it turns into a realistic schedule, with reminders when you need them.

No more losing a thought halfway because someone interrupted you. You can get it back later.

Nothing falls through the cracks. Mental Offload never forgets.
